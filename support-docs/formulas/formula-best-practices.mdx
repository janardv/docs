Good formulas are not only correct. They are also easy for another person to read, review, and safely change.

## Recommended practices

- Write one business rule per formula when possible.
- Use helper variables for repeated or layered logic.
- Name inputs clearly so references read like plain language.
- Check formulas at both detail and summary levels.
- Review actuals, forecast, and aggregation behavior before you publish.

## Optimize for maintenance

A model usually lives longer than its first builder expects. If a formula is hard to understand now, it will be much harder to trust later.
