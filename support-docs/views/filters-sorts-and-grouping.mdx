Filters, sorts, and grouping are what make a view useful instead of just smaller.

## Filters

Use filters to include only the variables, dimensions, scenarios, time periods, or charts relevant to the current question.

## Sorting

Use sorting to make the output easier to scan. This is especially useful when people review the same view regularly and expect the most important rows to stay visible.

## Grouping

Use grouping when the audience needs structure, such as reviewing the same metric by department or by product family.

## Keep the setup readable

A view should feel intentional. If it needs too many hidden rules to make sense, the underlying model or reporting approach may need cleanup.
