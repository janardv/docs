title: Sensitive data controls
description: Protect sensitive content with the right access and visibility controls.

---

Sensitive planning data needs more than a private link. It needs controlled access, predictable sharing rules, and a review habit that keeps exposure low.

## Useful controls

- least-privilege permissions
- restricted teamspaces or groups
- limited sharing of underlying models
- preference or UI controls that hide values during screen sharing
- audit and review processes for high-risk content

## Design for the audience

Not everyone needs the same level of detail. Share the highest-value summary surface first, then widen access only if the audience also needs the underlying model.

## Recheck after reorganization

When content moves between folders, teamspaces, or owners, review security assumptions again. Sensitive data risk often appears during reorganization, not during first creation.
