Comments are most useful when they keep planning discussion close to the source metric or report.

## Good collaboration habits

- comment on the specific item being reviewed
- describe the business question, not only the symptom
- resolve comments when the issue is closed
- use scenarios or views to support the discussion when needed

## Keep comments actionable

A good comment tells the next person what changed, what needs a response, or what decision is pending.

## Use comments with structured review

Comments work best when paired with named scenarios, shared views, or board reviews. That gives the discussion context and reduces back-and-forth.
