Sharing in Oculon works best when you match the audience to the right level of detail.

## What you can share

Teams typically share models, views, boards, and workspace resources. The right choice depends on whether the audience needs the full planning surface or only the outcome.

## Start with the narrowest useful surface

If stakeholders only need the result, share a board or a view. Share the full model only when the audience also needs to inspect or edit the planning logic.

## Confirm permissions before you send

Always review whether the audience can access the underlying content. A shared link is only useful if the viewer can actually open the required data.
