Roles and permissions control who can view, edit, manage, or administer content in Oculon.

## Roles define default responsibility

A role usually describes a person's broad level of responsibility in the workspace, such as builder, collaborator, or admin.

## Permissions define access on a resource

Permissions decide what a user can do on a specific model, board, database, or shared area. Two people can have the same workspace role but different access to a specific planning surface.

## Keep responsibility clear

The easiest permission model to manage is the one with clear owners, a small number of admins, and as much group-based access as possible.
