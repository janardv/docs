Charts help people see patterns faster than a raw grid can.

## When to use a chart

Use a chart when the decision depends on movement, comparison, or composition. Common examples include growth trends, scenario deltas, and category mix.

## Choose the chart for the question

- line charts for time trends
- bar charts for category comparison
- summary visuals for KPI monitoring

## Keep charts trustworthy

A good chart has clear labels, obvious units, and a time range the viewer can explain. Avoid styling choices that make small changes look larger than they are.
