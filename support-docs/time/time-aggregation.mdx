Time aggregation controls how values roll up from a lower time level to a higher one, such as month to quarter or quarter to year.

## Not every metric should aggregate the same way

A revenue line may sum across periods. A headcount line may need a point-in-time interpretation. A rate may need a weighted approach. Always match the aggregation rule to the business meaning of the metric.

## Where you see aggregation effects

You see time aggregation in model summaries, views, charts, and board-level tables. If a number looks wrong at a higher time level, aggregation is one of the first things to check.

## Review summary outputs

After you change time settings or formulas, review a quarterly and yearly summary view. That is the fastest way to catch an aggregation rule that no longer fits the metric.
