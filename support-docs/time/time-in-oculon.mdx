Time is a core modeling axis in Oculon. Every variable is evaluated over a date range, and the model uses that timeline to separate historical results from planned values.

## What time controls

Time affects:

- the start and end of the model
- how many periods the model contains
- how formulas behave across those periods
- how reports aggregate and compare values

## Why time setup matters early

Time decisions influence nearly everything else in the model. If you change date range or granularity after formulas, boards, and scenarios are already built, you usually need additional cleanup.

## What to confirm first

Before you build, confirm the planning horizon, the reporting cadence, the fiscal assumptions, and the point where actuals should stop.
