A clear model structure makes the model easier to build, test, and hand off.

![](/images/getting-started/fr-model-breakdown.png)

## Common structural layers

- sections for major business areas
- variables inside each section
- dimensions applied where detail is needed
- helper lines that support larger outputs
- summary lines or outputs that feed views and boards

## A practical organization pattern

Many teams organize a model in this order: assumptions, drivers, calculations, outputs. This keeps the logic readable and reduces circular editing between unrelated lines.

## Why structure matters for scale

A small model can survive weak structure. A shared model cannot. Once scenarios, linked models, and board reporting rely on the same logic, structure becomes a governance issue, not just a style choice.
