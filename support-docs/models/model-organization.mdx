---
title: Model organization
description: Organize models so teams can find, maintain, and collaborate on them more easily.
icon: folder-tree
---

Good model organization reduces duplicate work and makes ownership clear.

## Organize by business purpose

Use names, folders, and teamspaces that reflect how people search for planning work. A finance team should be able to tell at a glance which model is the operating forecast, which is the headcount plan, and which is an archived budget.

## Make ownership visible

Every important model should have a clear owner and a clear audience. That helps reviewers know where to send questions and who should approve major logic changes.

## Retire what is no longer active

Archive or label older models clearly so people do not build new work on top of stale logic.
