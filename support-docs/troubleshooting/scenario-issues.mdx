Scenario issues often come from confusion about inheritance, overrides, or merge behavior.

## Common symptoms

- a change appears in the wrong scenario
- a comparison shows more differences than expected
- baseline updates do not appear in a draft scenario
- an override remains after the reason for it is gone

## What to review

Check the active scenario, sync setting, override set, and the source-destination direction of any merge.
