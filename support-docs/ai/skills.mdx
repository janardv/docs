Skills are reusable packets of guidance that help AI behave more consistently in a specific kind of task. They are useful when the same planning pattern, documentation standard, or operating rule should be applied repeatedly.

## What a skill usually contains

A skill may define:

- the type of task it is for
- the structure of a good output
- rules or constraints the AI should follow
- references or examples that improve consistency

## Why skills matter

Without reusable guidance, AI outputs vary too much from one run to the next. Skills reduce that variation and make the workflow easier to scale across a team.

## Keep skills narrow

The best skills are focused. A skill for variance summaries is easier to trust than one giant skill that tries to cover every planning task at once.
