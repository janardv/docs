Plan mode is the part of the AI workflow where an idea turns into a working planning path. It helps you move from a prompt or rough question toward a set of structured next steps.

## What plan mode should help with

- breaking a large planning task into smaller actions
- clarifying the sequence of work
- surfacing assumptions before edits are made
- helping teams review a proposed approach before it becomes part of the model

## Why this matters

AI is most useful when it improves decision quality, not just speed. Plan mode creates a pause between suggestion and execution so teams can review the shape of the work first.

## Use it for structured iteration

Plan mode is especially useful when a task affects multiple parts of the workspace, such as models, boards, scenarios, or connected sources at once.
