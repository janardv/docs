AI in Oculon should help you move faster without separating the work from the planning context. The goal is not to add another tool to manage. The goal is to make modeling, analysis, reporting, and operational guidance easier inside the workspace you already use.

## Where AI can help

AI is most useful when it helps you:

- understand the model faster
- draft or refine planning logic
- summarize results for stakeholders
- navigate large workspaces and connected sources
- standardize repeatable workflows

## A useful way to think about AI

There are usually two kinds of AI help in a product like Oculon:

- built-in help inside the product workflow
- configurable help, such as custom agents, skills, or connections to external context

## What good AI behavior looks like

Good AI should stay grounded in the workspace context, respect permissions, and make its scope clear. If it is giving broad answers without clear context, it is usually not ready to support an important planning decision.
