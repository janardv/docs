Billing and plan settings determine how the workspace is licensed and which capabilities are available.

## What admins usually manage

- current plan and subscription details
- billing contact information
- plan changes or renewal decisions
- how seat or usage changes affect cost

## Connect billing to governance

Billing decisions often affect workspace growth, access, and feature rollout. Review billing in the context of actual planning usage, not as a separate admin task.

## Keep one source of ownership

A named billing owner reduces confusion when renewals, upgrades, or seat changes are needed quickly.
