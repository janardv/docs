As your workspace grows, structure matters. Teamspaces and similar shared areas help you separate content by team, workflow, or business purpose without losing visibility across the company.

![](/images/getting-started/fr-teamspace.png)

## What good organization does

Good organization helps people:

- find the current model or board quickly
- understand who owns a planning area
- avoid duplicate content
- keep sensitive work visible only to the right audience

## A simple organizing rule

Organize around how people work, not around temporary project names. `Finance`, `Revenue operations`, `Executive reporting`, and `Annual planning` are easier to understand than generic folders such as `Misc` or `Q3 temp`.

## When to create a separate shared area

Create a dedicated area when:

- one team owns the content
- access needs are different from the rest of the workspace
- the same models and boards are used repeatedly by one audience

## Keep the structure stable

Reorganize when needed, but do it deliberately. Frequent structural changes make it harder for people to trust where the latest source of truth lives.
