Workspace access determines who can enter the environment and what they can discover once they are inside it. Visibility controls shape what is easy to find, what is restricted, and how collaboration happens safely.

## Access starts at the workspace level

Before someone can use a model, board, or Transactions dataset, they need access to the workspace itself. That is why workspace membership, join rules, and identity controls matter so much.

Manage membership and invitations in [Users, guests and invitations](/concepts/settings-and-administration/users-guests-and-invitations). Workspace Owner and Member meanings live under [Roles and access](/concepts/settings-and-administration/roles-and-access).

## Visibility is not the same as edit access

Someone may be able to see that content exists without being able to edit it. In other cases, sensitive content may need to stay hidden unless a user is explicitly included.

Item-level **View**, **Edit**, and **Full Access** decide what people can do after they open a resource. See [Roles and permissions](/concepts/workspace-and-collaboration/sharing-and-permissions/roles-and-permissions).

## Spaces and discovery

[Spaces](/concepts/workspace-and-collaboration/teamspaces-and-private-space) organize content inside the workspace. Open or closed Space rules affect who can discover or request access to a shared area. They do not replace item sharing for every resource.

## Questions to answer early

- Who can join the workspace?
- Who can discover Spaces and shared areas?
- Which content should stay broadly visible?
- Which content should require explicit access?

Clear answers here make downstream sharing much easier.

## What this page does not cover

- Admin Security and Access configuration (login methods, audit logs)
- Billing or plan limits
- Deep share-dialog steps for every resource type

Those belong in Settings/Admin and Sharing docs.
