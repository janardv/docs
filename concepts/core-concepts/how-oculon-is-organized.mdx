Oculon isn't one strict tree. A workspace contains a few shared resources plus **Spaces** that hold your files. A **Library** lists everything you can access. An AI chat is available on every page. This page explains where each thing lives and how you move between them.

## Workspace

A workspace is your company's environment in Oculon. Everything else lives inside one.

These belong to the workspace as a whole, not to any single space:

| Resource | What it means |
| :--- | :--- |
| Members, groups, and roles | Who can enter the workspace and what they can administer |
| [Dimensions](/concepts/data-and-structure/dimensions) | Shared categories, such as `Region` or `Department`, that any model can use |
| Integrations | Connections to external systems that feed Transactions |
| [Scenarios](/concepts/modeling/scenarios) | Branches such as **Default Scenario** that you can apply across models |
| Settings | Planning defaults, security, billing, and other admin settings |

If you belong to more than one workspace, switch between them from the profile menu. See [Workspaces](/concepts/workspace-and-collaboration/workspaces).

## Spaces

Spaces are where your files live. There are two kinds:

- **Private Space**: your personal area. Only you can see it unless you share an item from it.
- **Teamspace**: a shared area for a team, such as Finance or Revenue Planning. A teamspace is either **Open** (anyone in the workspace can join) or **Closed** (people request to join).

A space holds models, Transactions, boards, and folders. You can nest folders to organize content further. See [Teamspaces and private space](/concepts/workspace-and-collaboration/teamspaces-and-private-space).

## Library

The Library is an index of everything you can access, not a separate place to store files. Its tabs list **Models**, **Transactions**, **Boards**, **Dimensions**, and **Integrations**. The **Location** column shows which space each file lives in.

Dimensions and integrations are the exception. They belong to the workspace, so the Library is where you manage them.

<Note>
When you create a model, Transactions, or a board from the Library, it is saved to your Private Space. Move it to a teamspace when others need it.
</Note>

## Inside a model

Many concepts you work with every day are parts of a model, not separate files:

- **Variables and formulas.** Each variable can have an **actual formula** and a **planned formula**.
- **Time settings.** Granularity, date range, and the **Last actual date** live in model settings.
- **Linked data.** The model links to Transactions, dimensions, and other models.
- **Views.** Saved views store a date range, filters, sorting, rollups, and comparison settings for that model.
- **Scenario and Compare controls.** The model header lets you switch scenarios and compare scenarios or time periods.

Transactions work the same way. Their saved views store filters, sorting, and grouping.

See [Models](/concepts/modeling/models) and [Views](/concepts/analysis-and-reporting/views).

## AI

AI isn't a separate area you navigate to. You open a chat from **New Chat** in the sidebar or with a keyboard shortcut from any page. The chat knows which page you're on, so you can ask about the model or board in front of you. Past conversations are on the **Chats** tab of the sidebar, and custom agents are on the **Team** tab. See [AI overview](/concepts/ai-and-agents/ai-overview).

## How you move around

The sidebar is the main way to get around:

| Sidebar area | What you use it for |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Search** | Jump to any file, chat, or setting you can access |
| **Home** tab | Library, Spaces, **Shared with me**, and **Recents** |
| **Chats** tab | Your AI conversation history |
| **Team** tab | Custom agents |
| Profile menu | **Settings**, **Switch Workspace**, and invitations |

Some items, such as **Projects** and **Feeds**, appear only when they are enabled for your workspace. See [Sidebar and search](/concepts/resources/sidebar-and-search).

## How access fits in

Access works at three levels:

1. **Workspace membership** decides who can enter the workspace at all.
2. **Teamspace membership** decides who sees a teamspace's content.
3. **Item sharing** gives a person or group access to a single model, Transactions, or board. You can share an item without adding the person to its space.

See [Sharing and permissions](/concepts/workspace-and-collaboration/sharing-and-permissions).
