Context and connections give AI access to the sources it needs to be useful. In Settings, this surface is labeled **Connectors & MCP** (also shown as **MCP & Connectors**).

This is different from Library **Integrations**, which connect source systems into planning data such as Transactions. Connectors and MCP ground AI workflows; Integrations feed planning data.

## Why connections matter

AI is most useful when it can work from the same trusted context your team already uses. Connections help bring that context into the workflow without forcing people to restate it manually every time.

## Good connection design

Good connections are:

- Clearly scoped
- Easy to review
- Aligned with workspace permissions
- Tied to a real business workflow

Workspace AI → **Tools & Connectors** can control who may add tools and whether external tools or MCP tools are allowed.

## Review connection impact

Whenever you add or expand a connection, check what new data becomes visible, what workflows it supports, and what governance expectations should change with it. Do not infer that AI automatically receives every workspace resource.
