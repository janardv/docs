AI in Oculon helps you move faster without separating the work from your planning context. The goal is not another tool to manage. The goal is to make modeling, analysis, reporting, and operational guidance easier inside the workspace you already use.

## Where AI can help

AI is most useful when it helps you:

- understand a model faster
- draft or refine planning logic
- summarize results for stakeholders
- navigate large workspaces and connected sources
- standardize repeatable workflows

## AI surfaces in Oculon

| Surface | What it is for |
| :--- | :--- |
| [AI assistant](/concepts/ai-and-agents/ai-assistant) | Chat help for modeling, reporting, and day-to-day questions |

| [Custom agents](/concepts/ai-and-agents/custom-agents) | Packaged instructions, context, and allowed actions for repeatable jobs |
| [Context and connections](/concepts/ai-and-agents/context-and-connections) | Connectors and MCP that ground AI in approved sources |
| [Knowledge and memory](/concepts/ai-and-agents/knowledge-and-memory) | Personal and agent knowledge that stays product-visible |

## Built-in help vs configurable help

There are usually two kinds of AI help:

- **Built-in help** inside the product workflow (the AI assistant)
- **Configurable help**, such as custom agents and connections to approved external context

## What good AI behavior looks like

Good AI should stay grounded in workspace context, respect permissions, and make its scope clear. If it gives broad answers without clear context, it is usually not ready to support an important planning decision.
