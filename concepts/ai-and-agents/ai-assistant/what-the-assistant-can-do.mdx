The AI assistant can do far more than answer questions. In Agent or Goal mode, it uses the same building blocks you do: models, variables, dimensions, scenarios, boards, and Transactions.

This page covers what the assistant can do, grouped by area. Everything here follows your permissions. If you can't see or edit a resource, the assistant can't either.

<Note>
  In Ask mode, the assistant can only read and explain. It switches to Agent mode, with your OK, before it makes changes. See [Chat basics](/concepts/ai-and-agents/ai-assistant/chat-basics).
</Note>

## Modeling

The assistant can inspect a model's structure, sections, and variables, and create new models, including from a template. It creates and edits variables and formulas, and it can check a formula and explain why it returns what it does. Try `Create a headcount model for FY27 with monthly granularity` or `Why does Net revenue show an error in March?`

It also creates dimensions and manages their values and links, and it creates and compares scenarios for what-if analysis, such as `Create a downside scenario with 10% lower bookings`. It can explain variances, lineage, and history.

For housekeeping, the assistant can copy models, move or duplicate sections and variables, and set model settings such as fiscal year and formats. See [Modeling with AI](/concepts/ai-and-agents/ai-assistant/modeling-with-ai) for more examples.

## Reporting

The assistant can create, rename, and inspect boards. It adds and configures charts, edits text blocks, sets board filters, and embeds model and Transactions blocks. Try `Add a line chart of ARR by region for the last 12 months` or `Write a short summary of this month's results at the top`.

See [Reporting with AI](/concepts/ai-and-agents/ai-assistant/reporting-with-ai) for more examples.

## Data and Transactions

The assistant can inspect Transactions tables and their columns, and add, update, or delete rows, columns, and cells. It can import data from an attached file, such as `Import the attached CSV into a new Transactions table`.

It also creates and runs queries, links Transactions to models, and triggers an integration sync and checks its status. Try `Sync the QuickBooks integration and tell me when it finishes`.

## Files and presentations

The assistant can build presentation decks from your data, edit and analyze spreadsheets, and run code for one-off analysis. Try `Turn the board into a 6-slide deck for the board meeting` or `Trace where cell D14 gets its value`.

## Collaboration and workspace

The assistant can add, reply to, and resolve comments, and create projects and tasks, such as `Create a task to review the FY27 hiring plan`. It can search the web for outside context and run your saved skills, for example `/variance-summary for October`.

It also remembers your preferences when you ask, such as `Remember that I report in USD thousands`.

## What the assistant cannot do

The assistant can't see or change resources you don't have access to, and it can't change workspace settings, billing, or members. It also can't skip approval for high-risk actions, such as deletes and access changes. See [Approvals and checkpoints](/concepts/ai-and-agents/ai-assistant/approvals-and-checkpoints).

To work with Oculon from Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor, connect the [Oculon MCP server](/concepts/ai-and-agents/context-and-connections/mcp).
