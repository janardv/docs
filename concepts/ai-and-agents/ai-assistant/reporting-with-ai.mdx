The AI assistant can turn model results into boards, charts, narratives, and presentation decks. Use it to shorten the time between a number changing and a clear explanation of why. For the full list of what it can do, see [What the assistant can do](/concepts/ai-and-agents/ai-assistant/what-the-assistant-can-do).

## Build a board

Describe the [board](/concepts/analysis-and-reporting/boards) you want, including the metrics and time range. For example, `Create a Monthly business review board with revenue, gross margin, and opex charts for the last 12 months`.

You can then refine it with follow-ups such as `Add a KPI block for ARR and net revenue retention` or `Filter the whole board to the EMEA region`.

## Explain variances

Name the comparison you want: scenario against scenario, or period against period. `Explain the variance between Budget and Actuals for Opex in October` compares scenarios for one period. `Which three accounts drove most of the change in Gross margin this quarter?` looks at change over time.

To check the numbers yourself, see [Scenario compare](/concepts/analysis-and-reporting/compare/scenario-compare) and [Time compare](/concepts/analysis-and-reporting/compare/time-compare).

## Write summaries

The assistant can draft narrative text for a board or a stakeholder. Try `Write a five-bullet summary of this month's results at the top of the board`, or `Draft a short note for the CFO on why cash burn increased`.

## Create a presentation deck

To build a deck, switch to [Plan mode](/concepts/ai-and-agents/ai-assistant/plan-mode) and ask for one, such as `Create an 8-slide board deck from the Monthly business review board`.

In Look and feel, set the slide count and pick a Deck theme. Then select **Generate**. The deck opens and fills in slide by slide.

## Review before you share

A clear summary is only useful if it reflects the right data. Before you share AI-written reporting, confirm the scenario, time range, and filters.

Check every number in the narrative against the chart or table, and remove any claim the data doesn't support.
