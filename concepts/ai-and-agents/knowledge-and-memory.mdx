Memory lets the [AI assistant](/concepts/ai-and-agents/ai-assistant) remember facts and preferences about your work, so you don't have to repeat them in every chat.

## Your memories

The assistant saves a memory when you ask it to, such as `Remember that I report in USD thousands`, or when you share a preference that's clearly useful later. It never saves passwords or other secrets, or text from files and web pages unless you say it in your own words.

Your memories are private to you and stay in the workspace where they were saved. To see or change them, open **Settings → My Account → AI**. You can add a memory, edit or delete one, or delete them all. You can also ask the assistant in chat to forget something.

Good memories are short and don't change often, such as `Our fiscal year starts in April.` For numbers that change every week, ask the assistant to read the model instead.

## Other context the AI uses

Admins can set shared instructions for everyone in [Workspace AI](/concepts/ai-and-agents/workspace-ai). Projects can hold their own instructions and memory for the AI working in that project. See [AI in projects](/concepts/workspace-and-collaboration/projects-and-tasks/ai-in-projects).

Each [custom agent](/concepts/ai-and-agents/custom-agents) has its own instructions, memory, and the resources shared with it, separate from yours. See [Create an agent](/concepts/ai-and-agents/custom-agents/create-a-custom-agent).

## Memory and permissions

Memory never widens access. The assistant and your agents can only read what you, or the agent, already have permission to see.
