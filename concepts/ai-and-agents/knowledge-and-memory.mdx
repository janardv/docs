Knowledge and memory help AI stay consistent across conversations when your workspace exposes those controls. Document only what you can see and manage in product settings.

## Personal memories

Under **My Account** AI settings, you can manage personal memories when the surface is available. Typical actions include editing or deleting a memory.

Personal memories apply to you. They are not a substitute for workspace policy in [Workspace AI](/concepts/ai-and-agents/workspace-ai).

## Agent knowledge

Custom agents can carry scoped knowledge and context you assign to them. Keep that knowledge narrow and current. See [Agent context and knowledge](/concepts/ai-and-agents/custom-agents/agent-context-and-knowledge).

## What this page does not claim

Do not assume:

- Automatic memory of every chat turn
- Workspace-wide shared memory unless a product control says so
- Project memory behavior unless Projects are enabled and documented

If a persistence or scope behavior is not visible in settings or agent configuration, treat it as undocumented.
