**Workspace AI** is the admin settings surface for how AI behaves across the workspace. Open it from Settings under Admin → **Workspace AI**.

This page is about workspace policy. Day-to-day chat lives in the [AI assistant](/concepts/ai-and-agents/ai-assistant). Packaged workflows live in [Custom agents](/concepts/ai-and-agents/custom-agents).

## Workspace AI tabs

Workspace AI is organized into:

| Tab | What you configure |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Defaults** | Workspace AI instructions and default AI behavior |
| **Agents** | Who can create, edit, share, and use shared agents |
| **Approvals** | Whether agents may take actions and related approval expectations |
| **Tools & Connectors** | Who can connect tools, and whether external tools or MCP tools are allowed |

## Model tier options

Workspace AI settings can include model tiers such as **Lite**, **Pro**, and **Max**. Use the tier that matches the sensitivity and quality needs of the workflow.

## Workspace AI instructions

Use **Workspace AI instructions** to set shared guidance that should apply across AI help in the workspace. Keep instructions short, explicit, and aligned with how your team plans.

## Keep policy separate from personal settings

- **Workspace AI** controls organization defaults and agent governance.
- **My Account** personal AI options (including personal memories) apply to you.
- Resource sharing and Space membership still decide what content a person or agent can reach.
