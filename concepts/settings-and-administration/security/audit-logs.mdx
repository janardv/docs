Audit and activity review help admins understand who changed what and whether follow-up is needed. In Concepts, treat this as an oversight capability—not a claim that every workspace has a dedicated Audit logs Settings page today.

## Current availability

A dedicated **Audit logs** browser is **not** confirmed in the current Settings overlay.

Do not document click-path admin instructions for an Audit logs screen that is not present. Prefer:

- Clear ownership of sensitive models and boards
- Careful use of [Sharing and permissions](/concepts/workspace-and-collaboration/sharing-and-permissions)
- Review of [Security and Access](/concepts/settings-and-administration/security) join rules
- Product activity surfaces that exist in your environment (for example comments, Model History, or Project activity where enabled)

## What audit-style oversight is for

When audit tooling is available on your plan or environment, teams typically use it to:

- Review permission changes
- Trace updates to important planning assets
- Support governance workflows
- Investigate unexpected behavior

## Pair logs with ownership

An audit trail is most useful when the resource owner and the admin can both interpret what changed and whether the change was expected.
