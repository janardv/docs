Usage and limits affect how much work the workspace can support and when admins should adjust structure or plan.

## What to monitor

In the current billing/usage view, admins can monitor:

- **Cells Used**
- **AI Credits**
- Breakdown of usage by model or related objects when shown

Limits are not only a billing issue. Rising usage can also signal cleanup, better model structure, or a different operating pattern.

## AI credits

**AI Credits** measure how much AI your workspace uses. Credits are counted over each billing period. They are used by:

- The AI assistant, including Plan and Goal mode
- Custom agent runs
- AI that builds presentations and other files

Larger models, such as **Pro** and **Max**, and longer tasks, such as goals, use more credits than quick questions on **Lite** or **Balanced**.

Your workspace's monthly credit allowance depends on your plan. Check the **AI Credits** card under **Settings → Admin → Usage** to see how many credits are used and how many remain. Anyone can also see **AI Credits** in the usage indicator in the sidebar.

### Get alerts before you run out

Turn on **Usage limits** under **My Account → Notifications → Workspace & Billing** to get an alert when AI credit or cell usage approaches the workspace limit.

## Review before teams hit a wall

Track usage early so you can adjust architecture, permissions, or plan decisions before a critical workflow is blocked.
