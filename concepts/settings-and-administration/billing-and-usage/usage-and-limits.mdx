Usage and limits affect how much work the workspace can support and when admins should adjust structure or plan.

## What to monitor

In the current billing/usage view, admins can monitor:

- **Cells Used**
- **AI Credits**
- Breakdown of usage by model or related objects when shown

Limits are not only a billing issue. Rising usage can also signal cleanup, better model structure, or a different operating pattern.

## Review before teams hit a wall

Track usage early so you can adjust architecture, permissions, or plan decisions before a critical workflow is blocked.
