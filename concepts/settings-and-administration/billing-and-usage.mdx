Billing and usage settings show how the workspace is licensed and how capacity is being consumed. Open them from Admin → **Plan and Billing** or **Usage** (Workspace Owners).

In the current Settings overlay, Plan and Billing and Usage open the same billing/usage view.

## What admins can review

Confirmed live concepts include:

| Area | What you see |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Current plan** | Active plan posture for the workspace |
| **Manage plan** | Entry to manage the subscription when available |
| **Cells Used** | Usage against planning/cell capacity |
| **AI Credits** | Usage against AI credit capacity |
| Model / object breakdown | Where records or usage concentrate (for example by object) |

Exact plan names, prices, and commercial terms are not documented here. Use the in-product billing surface and your commercial agreement for pricing.

## Connect billing to governance

Billing decisions often affect workspace growth, access, and feature rollout. Review usage before teams hit a hard limit, and keep one named billing owner for renewals and upgrades.
