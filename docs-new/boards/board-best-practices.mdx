The most effective boards are clear, restrained, and easy to maintain.

## Recommended practices

- Build each board around one primary audience.
- Lead with the KPI or chart that answers the top question.
- Use tables for support, not as the whole story.
- Keep filters and labels explicit.
- Review boards after model or scenario changes.

## Optimize for repeated reading

A board should become easier to use over time. If people need a walkthrough every month, simplify the structure.
