A board is a visual reporting surface in Oculon. It combines charts, tables, KPI blocks, and other summary elements so you can present model outputs clearly.

![](/images/getting-started/fr-boards.png)

## Create a board

Boards are the primary way to share insights and reports across your team. Creating one is a quick process that allows you to start building your presentation layer immediately.

### 1. Access the Boards list
To view your existing boards or start a new one, click on **Boards** in the left-hand navigation sidebar. This will open the main Boards library view, where you can see all boards you have access to.

### 2. Initialize a New Board

![](/images/getting-started/fr-create-board.png)

From the boards library, click the **+ New board** button located in the top-right corner. This will instantly create a fresh reporting surface and open it for editing.

### 3. Update the board name
New boards are created with the default title `Untitled`. To give your board a meaningful name:
- Navigate to the top header of the board.
- Click directly on the `Untitled` text to activate the edit mode.
- Enter your desired board name (e.g., "Monthly Executive Review") and press Enter.

### 4. Setting up your Board Canvas

![](/images/getting-started/fr-board-add-card.png)

Once your board is named, it acts as a blank canvas. You can start adding cards in it such as charts, tables, and KPI blocks—by selecting the `+` option in the canvas. Each card is powered by the logic and data from your underlying models.

### 5. Organization
For better team collaboration, you can move your board into a specific **Teamspace**. This ensures that the right groups of people have visibility and can contribute to the reporting process.


## Why boards matter

Boards turn planning logic into a decision-ready experience. They help stakeholders review the right metrics without opening the full model.

## What boards are best for

- Executive updates
- Monthly business reviews
- Team dashboards
- Scenario review packs
- Shared monitoring surfaces
