Comparison tools in Oculon allow you to evaluate your data across different dimensions of time and planning states. Whether you are looking at year-over-year growth or comparing your budget to your actuals, comparisons provide the context needed to make informed decisions.

## Types of comparisons

Oculon offers two main ways to compare your data:

- **[Time Compare](/docs-new/compare/time-compare)**: Analyze your data across different periods, such as current month vs. previous month.
- **[Scenario Compare](/docs-new/compare/scenario-compare)**: Compare different versions of your data, such as your "Base Case" vs. an "Aggressive Growth" scenario.

## Key features

- **Dynamic Variances**: Automatically calculate absolute and percentage variances between comparison points.
- **Multi-period/Scenario Support**: Compare your current view with one or more other periods or scenarios simultaneously.
- **Consistent Visuals**: Comparisons work seamlessly across models and boards, maintaining a consistent experience.

## Why use comparisons

In FP&A, data is most valuable when viewed in context. Comparisons allow you to:
- **Identify Trends**: See if performance is improving or declining over time.
- **Manage Risk**: Evaluate the potential impact of different scenarios.
- **Explain Variances**: Quickly pinpoint the cause of differences between expected and actual results.
