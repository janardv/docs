Time Compare allows you to compare models, boards, databases, and dimensions across different time periods. You can analyze current performance against historical data, such as the previous period, 3 months ago, or 6 months ago.

## Overview

You can compare the current period with one or more time periods simultaneously. This is useful for:
- Tracking growth trends (MoM, QoQ, YoY).
- Identifying seasonal patterns.
- Monitoring performance against historical benchmarks.

## Comparing in Models

In models, you can visualize time comparisons directly in your grid:

- **Rows and Columns**: Comparisons can be displayed in rows or columns depending on your layout preference.
- **Variances**: You can enable or disable **Variance** and **Variance %** calculations within the cells to see the absolute and relative differences between periods.

![Time Compare in Model](/images/getting-started/fr-time-compare.png)

## Comparing on Board Charts

On board charts, time comparisons are typically visualized as:
- **Bar charts**: Grouped bars showing data side-by-side for different periods.
- **Line charts**: Multiple lines representing different periods over time.

You can easily switch between these visualizations to find the best way to represent your data.
