Sidebar customization helps you adapt the workspace to the way you work without changing the underlying model structure.

## Common customization areas

- collapsed or expanded navigation state
- favorites and pinned items
- teamspace or section visibility based on role
- personal working patterns such as recent items

## Use customization to reduce friction

Customization should help you reach important content faster. It should not hide the structure so much that collaboration becomes inconsistent.

## Remember the difference between personal and shared structure

Your personal shortcuts are useful for you. Shared naming and organization are what help the rest of the team.
