Roles and permissions work together in Oculon, but they do different jobs.

## Start with two layers

Space and governance controls decide who can discover or manage a shared area. Item-level permissions decide what a person can do on a specific model, board, page, or view.

That means two people can sit in the same workspace or teamspace but still have different access to the exact same planning asset.

## Use one item-level access model

Oculon item access is easiest to understand when you use three levels:

| Access level | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `View` | Read-only access. The person can open what is shared and use what is already exposed, but cannot make changes. |
| `Edit` | Can change inputs or content without taking over governance. This level does not include permission management or other admin-style controls. |
| `Full access` | Can make structural changes, manage sharing, move content, and control permission settings on that item. |

## How this applies to models

- `View`: Open the model and review visible data.
- `Edit`: Change inputs and create draft changes.
- `Full access`: Publish structural changes, manage formulas and relationships, move the model into a teamspace, and manage access.

Use model access carefully. It affects the planning surface directly, not just the presented output.

## How this applies to boards and pages

- `View`: Open the board or page and use what is already exposed.
- `Edit`: Change layout, blocks, and local filters, and share the board or page directly with people or groups.
- `Full access`: Manage permission settings and link sharing, align dependency access, and publish or move the item into a teamspace.

Boards and pages do not raise source permissions on their own. If the underlying sources are restricted, the audience may still see limited results. For more on that behavior, review `Sharing basics`.

## How this applies to views

- `View`: Open the saved slice and review the data already exposed through that view.
- `Edit`: Work within the visible slice when editing is allowed.
- `Full access`: Change the saved view definition and manage view sharing.

A view can narrow what someone sees, but it does not widen access to the underlying source. For the current collaboration workflow, review `Sharing views`.

## Keep governance and access aligned

Clear ownership still matters. Keep the number of people with `Full access` small, prefer group-based sharing where it fits, and review inherited access whenever content moves between shared areas.
