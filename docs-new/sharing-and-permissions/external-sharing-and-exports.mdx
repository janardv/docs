External sharing and exports are useful when people need information from Oculon but do not work inside the platform every day.

## Decide what leaves the workspace

Before you export or share externally, decide whether the recipient needs the live planning surface or only a snapshot of the output.

## Common export use cases

- board packs for executive review
- tables for offline analysis
- controlled snapshots for audit or approval workflows

## Protect context when exporting

An exported number can lose the filters, scenario, and time assumptions that created it. Include enough context so the recipient understands what they are looking at.
