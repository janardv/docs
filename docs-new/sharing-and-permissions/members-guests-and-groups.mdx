Oculon access usually involves a mix of direct members, limited external participants, and groups that scale access across teams.

![](/images/getting-started/fr-permissions-list.png)

## Members

Members are regular workspace participants. They are usually part of the main planning and reporting workflow.

## Guests

Guests are best for limited collaboration where a person should only see a defined slice of content rather than the full workspace.

## Groups

Groups make permissions easier to maintain over time. Instead of updating access one person at a time, assign permissions to a group and let membership changes flow through.
