Model settings define the rules the rest of the model depends on. They usually control time, actuals, structure defaults, and model-wide behavior.

![Last actual date and time settings in Oculon](/images/getting-started/fr-last-actual.png)

## Settings to review early

- model date range
- [Time granularity](/docs-new/time/time-granularity)
- [Last actual date](/docs-new/time/last-actual-date)
- fiscal year assumptions
- naming and visibility defaults where applicable

## Why settings are important

Changing a model setting later can affect formulas, boards, and comparisons. Confirm the basics before you build deep logic or share the model widely.

## Review after major changes

If a model starts to behave unexpectedly, check the settings before you rewrite formulas. Many issues begin with a model-wide assumption that drifted over time.

For time-related behavior, start with [Time in Oculon](/docs-new/time/time-in-oculon) and then review the specific pages for granularity, actuals cutoff, aggregation, and rollups.
