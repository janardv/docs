A shared view is the supported way to give people a focused slice of a model or database without sending them to the full source.

![View sharing panel in Oculon](/images/getting-started/fr-share-views.png)

## When to share a view

Share a view when people need:

- a recurring review surface
- team-specific access to one slice of the source
- a reusable filtered definition for planning or data review
- a cleaner handoff than the full model or database

## Private views are not yet supported

Oculon does not support private views yet. Treat saved views as team-facing assets, and create them when the definition is ready to be reused or reviewed by others.

## Review before you share

Before you share a view:

- confirm the filters show only the intended content
- confirm the source model or database is current
- confirm the audience can access the source and any linked board context they need
- preview the view the way the audience will use it

## Choose the right surface

Share a view when people need filtered detail. Share a board when they mainly need a curated presentation of the result.
