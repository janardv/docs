Views are often the easiest place to compare outputs because they let you narrow the data before you ask for a variance.

## Good comparison use cases

- scenario versus scenario
- actual versus forecast
- current month versus prior month
- one dimension slice versus another

## How to keep comparisons useful

Compare one business question at a time. If the view is already filtered to the right variables and time periods, the difference becomes much easier to explain.

## Check context first

If a comparison looks wrong, confirm the active scenario, time aggregation, and dimension filters before you assume the formula is incorrect.
