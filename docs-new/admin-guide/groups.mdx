Groups help you scale access management without recreating the same permissions for every person.

## Why groups are useful

Groups reduce manual updates, keep access aligned with team structure, and make audits easier.

## Good group design

Create groups around stable business concepts such as Finance, RevOps, or Executive Review. Avoid temporary groups that mirror a one-time meeting or short-lived project unless the access need is truly temporary.

## Review group membership regularly

A clean group is only useful if the membership stays current. Review ownership and membership as part of onboarding and offboarding.
