Usage and limits affect how much work the workspace can support comfortably and what needs closer governance.

## What to monitor

Admins usually monitor resource growth, model size, refresh volume, collaboration demand, and any plan-linked usage thresholds.

## Why limits matter

Limits are not only a billing issue. They also signal where a workspace may need cleanup, better structure, or a different operating pattern.

## Review before teams hit a wall

Track usage early so you can adjust architecture, permissions, or plan decisions before a critical workflow is blocked.
