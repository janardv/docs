Workspace settings control how the environment is named, accessed, and governed.

![](/images/getting-started/fr-workspace-set.png)

## What workspace settings usually cover

- workspace name and identity
- join or access policy
- general environment defaults
- destructive actions such as archive or delete

## Review settings centrally

Changes at the workspace level can affect every team using Oculon. Make them in a controlled way and communicate when the change affects access or workflow.

## Keep ownership clear

A small number of accountable workspace admins is easier to manage than many partially responsible owners.
