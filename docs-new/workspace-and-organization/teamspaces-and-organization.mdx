As your workspace grows, structure becomes critical. Teamspaces help you organize models, boards, and databases by team, function, or use case—while keeping collaboration and access control clean.


## Accessing Teamspaces

![](/images/getting-started/fr-spaces-icon.png)

You can access teamspaces from:

- the **left navigation bar**
  - collapsed state → click the **grid icon**
  - expanded state → under **Spaces**
- the **profile menu → Switch workspace / space**

Every user starts with a **Private Space**, where personal work is stored. This space is not shared with others.


## Why Teamspaces matter

Teamspaces help you:

- separate planning areas (e.g., Finance, RevOps, Sales)
- control access to sensitive models (budgets, forecasts)
- avoid clutter and duplicate work
- create a clear ownership structure

Typical teamspace setup:

- **Finance** → budgeting, forecasting, actuals
- **Revenue / GTM** → pipeline, ARR, CAC models
- **Executive** → dashboards and reporting
- **Scenario planning** → what-if and simulations


## Creating a Teamspace

![](/images/getting-started/fr-create-space.png)

To create a new teamspace:

1. Click **+** next to **Spaces**
2. Enter:
   - **Teamspace name**
   - **Description**
   - **Join permission**
     - **Open** → anyone can join
     - **Closed** → invite only
3. Click **Create**

You can also create **folders inside a teamspace** to further organize content.


## Managing Teamspaces

Open a teamspace and use the **••• (more options)** menu to access settings.

Teamspace settings are organized into **three main tabs**:

- **General** → basic settings, permissions, and lifecycle actions  
- **Members** → manage users and roles  
- **Security** → control who can perform critical actions  


## General

### General settings

![](/images/getting-started/fr-space-general.png)

- Update **name and description**
- Change **join permissions** (open / closed)

### Permissions & access

Teamspaces allow you to define what different roles can do.

#### Roles

- **Teamspace Owner** → full control  
- **Teamspace Member** → access based on permissions  

#### Access levels

You can configure:

- **View** → read-only  
- **Edit** → modify content  
- **Full access** → manage content and settings  

### Leaving or deleting a Teamspace

- **Leave teamspace** - Removes it from your sidebar  
- **Delete teamspace** - Permanently deletes all content  

> Note:  
> You cannot leave a teamspace if you are the **only member**.  
> Add another member first—ownership will be reassigned if needed.


## Members

![](/images/getting-started/fr-space-mem.png)

From the **Members tab**:

- Add users from the workspace  
- Assign roles:
  - Owner  
  - Member  
- Control who has access to the teamspace  


## Security

![](/images/getting-started/fr-space-sec.png)

From the **Security tab**, you can define:

- Who can **invite and manage members**  
- Who can **change teamspace settings**  
- Who can **delete the teamspace**  

This ensures governance, especially for sensitive financial data.


## Best practices

- Organize by **function**, not temporary projects  
  (e.g., “Finance” instead of “Q2 Planning Temp”)  
- Keep **sensitive models** in restricted teamspaces  
- Use folders inside teamspaces for **structured workflows**  
- Avoid frequent restructuring to maintain a **single source of truth**  


A well-structured workspace ensures teams can move faster, collaborate better, and trust their data.
