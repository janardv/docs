A workspace is the top-level environment where your team uses Oculon. It brings together people, permissions, planning assets, and shared settings so work happens in one governed place instead of across disconnected files.

## What lives in a workspace

A workspace usually includes:

- the people who collaborate in Oculon
- shared models, databases, views, and boards
- workspace-level settings and access rules
- team structures such as groups or shared areas

## Why the workspace matters

The workspace is the frame around everything else. It determines who can join, what they can see, and how content is organized. If the workspace is set up well, navigation and collaboration both become easier.

## How to think about it

If a model is where planning logic lives, the workspace is where planning operates. It is the shared environment that makes that logic usable across a team.
