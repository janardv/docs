Time granularity defines the level of detail used for each period in the model. Most planning models use monthly granularity, but the right choice depends on the decisions you need to support.


## Common granularity choices

- **Monthly**: best for most FP&A workflows and rolling forecasts.
- **Quarterly**: useful for higher-level planning or executive reporting.
- **Yearly**: useful for long-range planning with low operational detail.

## How to choose

Use the most detailed level that still supports how the business reviews performance. If the business works month by month, start there. If the model is strategic only, quarterly may be enough.

## The tradeoff

More detail gives you finer control, but it also increases model size and maintenance. Choose a granularity that helps decisions, not just one that feels more precise.
